Privacy Policy

Personal CRM · published by BITSCOL · effective 24 September 2026 · version 1.0.0

No data collected by the publisher

The short version: Personal CRM is built to work with no server, no account and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We (BITSCOL) do not receive, see or store any of your contacts, tasks, notes or events — we have no server that could receive them. The only data that ever leaves your device is data you explicitly send to an account you own, using optional features you switch on yourself.

2. What the app stores, and where

All app content is stored locally on your device (in the app's own storage area):

  • Contacts — names, phone numbers, email addresses, birthdays, anniversaries, notes, tags, groups, social links, gift ideas, interests
  • Tasks, calendar events, notes, tags and groups
  • App settings, widget layout, notification preferences and the sync/backup configuration
  • An audit log of imports, syncs and changes you make, so you can review or roll them back

This data never leaves the device unless you use one of the optional features in section 3.

4. Registration, verification and what we receive

On first launch the app asks for your name and email (mobile optional) so it can greet you personally and support you if you lose access. That profile is stored on your device. Two things can optionally leave it:

  • Registration notice. If you leave the checkbox ticked, your name, email and mobile are sent once to BITSCOL so we know the app is registered. Untick it and nothing is sent — registration still works.
  • Verification codes. If you ask to verify your email or mobile, the app generates a one-time 6-digit code and sends it to us so we can forward it to that address or number. Only a salted hash of the code is kept on your device, never the code itself. Entering it confirms the address or number is yours. Verification is recorded locally — it is not a central account and does not identify you to anyone else.

These messages are delivered by a third-party form/email service acting as a processor for BITSCOL. We use the details only to register your copy, verify your contact details and reply to support requests. We never sell them and never use them for marketing. Email sales@bitscol.com at any time to have them deleted.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics or telemetry. There is no usage tracking, no screen recording, no behavioural profiling.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party trackers embedded in the app.
  • No account required. We have no sign-up, so we hold no email address, password or profile for you.
  • No sale or sharing of data of any kind.

6. App lock (pincode)

If you set a pincode, it is converted to a salted SHA-256 hash and stored only on your device. The plain pincode is never stored, never transmitted and cannot be recovered by us — if you forget it, the only way back into the app is to erase its local data and start fresh. The pincode is a screen lock: it stops someone casually opening the app on your device. It is not encryption of the stored database, and it is not a substitute for device-level security (a device passcode and full-disk encryption).

7. Device backups and uninstalling

Your operating system may include app data in its own backup (for example, Android's cloud backup). Where the platform allows, we restrict this; you can also disable it in your device settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. You can delete everything yourself at any time from Settings → Local data → Reset everything to blank, or export a copy first with Download all data.

8. Children

Personal CRM is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends largely on that device: keep it locked with a passcode or biometrics, keep the OS updated, and use the in-app pincode if others can reach your phone. We recommend taking periodic backups using the export feature.
